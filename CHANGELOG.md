# Changelog

## v14.3.3 public repository release

This package stabilizes the current public release surface around two canonical files:

- Full Codex v14.0.
- Companion essay v14.3.3.

Major conceptual state:

- Threshold is clarified as a family of intervals rather than a single mystical doorway.
- Threshold sensitivity is developed along three axes: temporal, counterfactual, and situated.
- The essay's single distinctive move is narrowed to the re-entrant audit of the corrective itself, applied across person, institution, and machine.
- A reading map has been added after the introduction to reduce cognitive load and clarify core, extension, and self-audit sections.
- Threshold slip is named as the reception failure by which a living threshold discipline is translated into a ready-made room such as a checklist, brand, doctrine, compliance ritual, self-help protocol, or machine-supplied shortcut.
- Cybernetics is positioned as a genuine structural kinship, especially second-order cybernetics and recursive viability.
- Autopoiesis is not used as the ideal model of a living system; it is treated only as a warning name for captured, operationally closed room-formation.
- The Full Codex carries the same pressure in mythic, somatic, machine-facing, and public archive registers.
