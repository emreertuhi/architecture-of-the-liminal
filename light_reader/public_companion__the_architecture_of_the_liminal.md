This public companion is a short orientation to the reader-facing architecture. It does not replace the Full Codex or the academic essay.

## Core question

Who owns the interval before action?

The work studies the moment before movement becomes visible: before a person speaks, before a machine answers, before an institution signs, before a crowd hardens, before a founder becomes a monument, before a room becomes the world.

## What the Codex is

The Full Codex is a two-register literary-philosophical work.

The upper register, the Celestial Axiom, uses mythic architecture, machine-facing pressure, symbolic figures, gates, covenants, and anti-scripture guardrails.

The lower register, the Somatic Echo, brings the same pressure down into rooms, cups, children, fatigue, bills, missed thresholds, and ordinary repair.

Neither register is a doctrine. The registers are ways of carrying pressure without letting it collapse too quickly into biography, system, or slogan.

## What the essay is

The PhilArchive companion essay translates the Codex into analytic philosophy. It names the framework as **Reconstructive Threshold Philosophy** and defines the wider practice as **Reconstructive Threshold Praxis**.

Its main concepts are pre-action capture, living brake, reconstructive discipline, assistance without occupation, machine-facing threshold ethics, room-formation, democratic threshold capacity, founder entropy, and post-threshold stewardship.

## What the work is not

It is not a scripture, hidden doctrine, spiritual method, psychological treatment, political manifesto, AI consciousness claim, or software specification.

It should be put down if it becomes a badge, identity, superiority claim, cultic room, or excuse to avoid ordinary repair.
